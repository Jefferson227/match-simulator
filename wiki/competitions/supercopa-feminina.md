# Supercopa Feminina — 2026

**Not seeded**, but the cheapest cup to add: one tie, one leg, two clubs the game already has, no
bracket. The natural first cup to implement once knockouts exist. See [[ms-102-cups-deferred]].

**Qualification (REC Supercopa Feminina 2026, Art. 2º + Art. 11).** Critério 1: champion of the
Campeonato Brasileiro Feminino A1 **2025**. Critério 2: champion of the Copa do Brasil Feminina
**2025**. §1º: if one club won both, the opponent is the **A1 2025 runner-up**.

> The 2026-09-05 pass cited "A1 REC Art. 5º" for this. The rule's substance is the same, but that
> article governs the **2027** edition (A1 2026 champion vs Copa 2026 champion) — as does Art. 5º of
> the Copa 2026 REC. For the **2026** edition the authoritative citation is the Supercopa's own REC,
> Arts. 2º and 11.
